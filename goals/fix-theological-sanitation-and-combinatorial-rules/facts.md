# Facts: Sanitize Theological Terms and Prevent Disrespectful Word Combinations

## Architectural Invariants & Constraints
- Halal naming compliance: Identifiers generated by this library must remain dignified, inspiring, and free of blasphemous, insulting, or racially sensitive connotations.
- In Sunni Islamic tradition (*Ahl al-Sunnah wa al-Jama'ah*), names of Allah exclusive to the Divine must never be attributed to created beings without the servant prefix (*Abd*).
- Eschatological integrity: Paradise (*Jannah*) is eternal; concepts implying an end to Paradise (`jannah-end`) violate theological doctrine.
- Prophetic titles (`muzammil`, `mudathir`, `amin`, `sadiq`) and companion titles (`faruq`) must never be paired with animals.

## File & Interface Contracts
- `words.json`: `categories.nouns_concepts`, `categories.suffixes`, `categories.muslim_names_male`, `categories.saudi_food`, `categories.arabic_food`, and `rules` array.
- `halal-random-strings_test.go`: Add tests ensuring safe pairings and absence of excluded terms.
